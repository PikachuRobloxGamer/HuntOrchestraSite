    Concert day guide

    Dress
    All black: black dress shirt or blouse, black pants or long skirt, black socks and closed-toe shoes.
    Arrive
    45 minutes before start time, instrument tuned and rosined, music in a black folder.
    On stage
    Walk on quietly, sit tall, watch the director. Instruments up and down together.
    Audience
    Silence phones. Clap at the end of a whole piece, not between movements. Enter and leave between pieces.
    After
    Stay until the last ensemble finishes. Every group deserves a full house.

    Composer spotlight

    Antonín Dvořák
    1841 – 1904 · Bohemia (today's Czech Republic)

    Dvořák started out playing viola in an opera orchestra in Prague, so he knew string sections from the inside. He later moved to New York to lead a music school and wrote his famous "New World" Symphony there in 1893.

    • He spent a summer in Spillville, Iowa, where he wrote his "American" String Quartet.
    • He loved trains and liked to memorize locomotive numbers.
    • His Serenade for Strings is a favorite for school orchestras.

      Join

      How to sign up

      Orchestra counts as a fine arts credit and runs all year. Students can join as beginners in 6th grade or audition in at any grade.

      Ask a director
      1. Pick Orchestra on course selectionChoose Beginning Strings on the Frisco ISD course request form in spring.
      2. Come to instrument fitting nightTry violin, viola, cello and bass with a director. You leave with a recommendation and the right size.
      3. Rent or borrow an instrumentRent-to-own from a local music store, or ask about school-owned cellos and basses.
      4. Get your suppliesMethod book, rosin, shoulder rest or rock stop, and a folding stand for home practice.
      5. Show up on day oneNo experience needed. We start with posture and bow hold and play our first song within weeks.

      For parents

      Frequently asked questions

      Does my child need experience to join?

      No. Beginning Strings starts from zero. Most of our players picked up an instrument for the first time in 6th grade.

      How much does orchestra cost?

      The main costs are an instrument rental, a method book and basic supplies. Ask the directors about school-owned instruments if cost is a concern. Donations to the Orchestra Fundraiser 2026 help keep loaners available.

      How much should my child practice?

      About 20 minutes a day, five days a week. Short daily practice beats one long session. The Practice Room above has a timer and log.

      Can my child do orchestra and athletics?

      Yes. Many students do both. Concert dates come out early in the year so families can plan around games.

      What if we move up a size mid-year?

      Students grow. Most rental programs let you trade up to the next size at no extra charge. Directors check fit each semester.

      How can I help?

      Join the Booster Club, volunteer at concerts and give to the fundraiser. Ask your employer about matching gifts to double your donation.
